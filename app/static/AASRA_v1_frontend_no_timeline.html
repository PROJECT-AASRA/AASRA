TOOLBAR BUTTONS & EMOJIS EXPLAINED
🧭
Front Page Navigation
Home & District Selector
Refers to: The AASRA portal landing page.
What it does: Exits the live map dashboard and returns to the home page where you can choose a different state or district and access regional hazard briefings.
👉 Click to navigate back home
⚡
Hazard Telemetry
Disaster Telemetry Layer
Refers to: Multi-hazard GIS intelligence.
What it does: Toggles visibility of 1,500+ surveyed landslide events (red dots) and active Himalayan river channels (cyan lines) on and off.
👉 Click button to show / hide hazard layers
🏕️
Relocation Shelters
Evacuation Safe Havens
Refers to: 263 emergency relief facilities and safe zones.
What it does: Toggles green shelter markers on/off. Clicking any shelter displays bed capacity, live occupancy, and medical support readiness.
👉 Click button to show / hide shelters
🏠
Vulnerable Habitations
Habitations & Settlements
Refers to: Surveyed mountain hamlets and vulnerable households.
What it does: Toggles habitation markers. Clicking any settlement focuses the map and calculates a route to the nearest safe shelter.
👉 Click button to show / hide habitations
🔄
Recenter Camera
Map View Reset
Refers to: Geographic viewpoint reset.
What it does: Smoothly flies the map camera and zoom level back to the centered district coordinates (e.g., Chamoli, Uttarakhand).
👉 Click button to reset camera
ℹ️
Controls & Legend
Interactive Manual
Refers to: This interactive on-screen operational manual.
What it does: Opens or closes this comprehensive modal guide explaining all toolbar tools, emojis, and map symbology.
👉 Press Esc or click ✕ to close
MAP SYMBOLOGY & COLOR CODES
Red Circle Critical Hazard Zone & Landslide: Active ground instability, slope displacement, or high-priority evacuation habitation (>80% risk).
Amber Circle High Exposure Habitation: Monitored settlement with elevated risk (60–79% vulnerability score).
Green Circle Relocation Shelter / Safe Haven: Verified emergency camp outside hazard footprint, with live bed capacity and supplies.
Cyan Line River Channel / Flood Basin: Major river courses (Alaknanda, Mandakini, Dhauliganga) monitored for surges.
Dashed Cyan AI Evacuation Corridor: Generated route directing high-risk residents to the closest suitable shelter.
💡 Tip: You can also hover over any toolbar icon anytime for instant tooltips.
